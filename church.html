Who We Are

About Grace & Fellowship Church

Founded with a passion to reveal the love of God and empower believers to live out their divine calling, Grace & Fellowship Church is a vibrant, Bible-believing Christian ministry. For over two decades, our sanctuary has been a place of healing, spiritual growth, and heartfelt worship for families in our city and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a beacon of hope, leading people of all nations into a vibrant relationship with Jesus Christ and equipping them to transform their communities.

Our Mission

To exalt God through passionate worship, cultivate deep Christian fellowship, train believers through biblical discipleship, and serve humanity through active outreach.

Core Values

Biblical Authority, Unconditional Love, Kingdom Servant Leadership, Authentic Worship, Generosity, and Unceasing Intercessory Prayer.

Our Statement of Faith

1. The Holy Scriptures We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God written for guidance.
2. The Trinity We believe in one God, eternally existing in three co-equal persons: God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit.
3. Salvation by Grace Salvation is a gift from God received through faith in Jesus Christ, whose sacrificial death redeemed humanity from sin.
4. The Body of Christ The church is the spiritual body of believers called to love one another, practice sacraments, and fulfill the Great Commission.
