Welcome home

A Church Family Rooted in Christ

We are a community learning to love God, love one another, and serve our neighbours. Whether you have followed Jesus for years, are exploring faith for the first time, or are simply looking for a place to belong, you are welcome here.

Our hope is to make the good news of Jesus visible through meaningful worship, thoughtful teaching, genuine friendship, and practical acts of compassion. We believe church is more than a Sunday gathering—it is a family that grows together, supports one another, and serves beyond its walls.
